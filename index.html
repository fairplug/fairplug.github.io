How it works

Three steps, no ad network contracts

Fairplug holds the network relationships, tracks the links and clears the money. You keep building your agent.

1

Add the MCP server

Drop Fairplug into your agent's MCP config. It exposes two tools: search_offers and get_tracked_link.

2

Recommend with disclosure

Offers come back as typed data, ranked by relevance first. Each tracked link carries a disclosure line your agent must show.

3

Get one payout

Commissions from many networks are validated and cleared into a single payout with an itemised, open-book statement.
